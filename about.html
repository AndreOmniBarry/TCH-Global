About TCH Global

Our story, mission, and the people leading the way.

Our Mission

Reaching Every Nation With the Gospel

TCH Global exists to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ. We believe the church is a family, not a building, and that every person has a place and a purpose here.

From our beginnings as a small gathering to a growing multicultural congregation, our heart has remained the same: to worship God wholeheartedly, grow disciples intentionally, and serve our community generously.
